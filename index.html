Since 1983

The MadamCass Legacy

MadamCass Restaurant was established with a clear mandate: to bring home-style cooking and premium Nigerian dining into clean, vibrant, accessible spaces across the nation.

Over the last 40 years, we have pioneered restaurant standards, created iconic dishes such as our celebrated Noble Rice and traditional Ofada delicacies, and built an enduring brand loved by generations.

Authentic Recipes

We preserve traditional cooking techniques using authentic spices and fresh local produce.
